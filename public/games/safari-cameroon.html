Cameroon, the edge of the map. You are Dr. Alaa Abdel Azim, a young Egyptian doctor newly posted to the Safari medical unit. Drums have been beating in the northern village since dawn — the elders whisper of Dawa, black magic sent up the river. But fevers are not cast by witches. Find the truth before the jungle goes dark.
